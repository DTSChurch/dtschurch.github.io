Overflow Gateway

Bring Overflow giving into Rock RMS

Overflow Gateway connects the Overflow giving platform — which specializes in cryptocurrency, stock, and donor-advised-fund (DAF) gifts — to your Rock database. A scheduled job pulls donors, recurring gifts, and contributions out of Overflow and records them in Rock’s financial system, so your contribution statements, batches, and giving reports stay complete without any manual re-entry.

Custom Plugin Rock RMS 16.13+ Financial Gateway Scheduled Job

The Two Halves

The plugin installs two cooperating pieces.

Financial Gateway

Overflow Financial Gateway

Registers Overflow as a payment gateway in Rock and stores your API credentials, batching preferences, and the fallback financial account. It’s also the connection the sync job uses to reach the Overflow API.

Scheduled Job

Overflow Sync Job

The heart of the plugin. On a schedule you choose (daily at 8:15 AM by default), it pulls the latest donors, subscriptions, and contributions from Overflow and writes them into Rock as people, scheduled transactions, transactions, and batches.

What It Syncs

People

Overflow donors are matched to existing Rock people (or created if new) and linked with a durable connection so future runs update the same record.

Recurring Gifts

Overflow subscriptions become Rock scheduled transactions, including the saved payment method and giving detail.

Contributions

Individual gifts — cash, card, ACH, stock, and crypto — post as Rock financial transactions, allocated to the right account and grouped into batches.

How Data Flows

1. Connect the gateway

Enter your Overflow API Key and Client ID on the Overflow Financial Gateway, and set your batching preferences and a fallback account.

2. Map your campaigns

On the Campaign Maps page, link each Overflow campaign (and subcampaign) to a Rock Financial Account so gifts land in the right fund.

3. Let the job run

The Overflow Sync Job runs on its schedule (or on demand via Run Now) and walks four stages: People → Subscriptions → Transactions → Batching.

4. Review the summary

Each run reports created / updated / skipped counts per stage, plus a Skip Reason Breakdown with names and amounts so you can see exactly what didn’t map.

Direction of Sync

The primary direction is Overflow → Rock — Overflow is the source of truth for giving data, and Rock is the destination. The gateway component also supports a limited Rock → Overflow path for actions like cancelling a subscription or initiating a refund.

  • Idempotent. Re-running the job updates existing records instead of creating duplicates — each contribution is keyed by its Overflow ID.
  • Resilient. One bad record doesn’t stop the run; it’s counted as a skip with a readable reason and the job keeps going.
  • Rolling window. The transactions stage looks back a configurable number of days (30 by default), while people and subscriptions always pull in full.
